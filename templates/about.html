JA4+ Network Fingerprinting Suite

The JA4+ signature suite

JA4+ is a suite of network fingerprinting standards created and maintained by FoxIO. Each method distils a protocol handshake into a short, human- and machine-readable fingerprint for threat hunting, detection engineering, and attribution. JA4LookR decodes and looks up these fingerprints entirely offline against a bundled JA4DB snapshot.

Credit The JA4+ standard, the reference diagrams below, and the JA4 database are the work of FoxIO (BSD 3-Clause / patent-pending). JA4LookR is an independent offline companion tool — all credit for the fingerprinting methods goes to FoxIO. See the FoxIO-LLC/ja4 repository.

How JA4LookR works

1

Detect the type

The input is auto-classified into its JA4+ member — JA4, JA4S, JA4H, JA4X, JA4T, JA4TScan, JA4SSH, JA4D/JA4D6 — from its structure.

2

Decode the signature

Each segment is broken out and labelled: protocol, TLS version, SNI, ciphers, extensions, ALPN, hashes, and per-type fields.

3

Match & score

The fingerprint is matched against the offline JA4DB (exact / near / cipher-variant / wildcard), and JA4 clients are risk-scored for C2 indicators.

4

Pivot & export

Optionally pivot JA4/JA4S through VirusTotal, generate a YARA hunting rule, and export the full result as JSON.
